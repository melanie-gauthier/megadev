The 5 one-day moves are counted under parser, where the desk files them by tenant, and have an issue of their own.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month swap. Any channel, any form.
  • hint: booking-v3's form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads it day first. Only the form channel of booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day move: the booking lands a day early, or on different days in different time zones. Nothing is swapped. These are the reports the desk already noted as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The desk counts them under parser, where their tenant files them, so the parser row includes them; they have an issue of their own.

Where they stand

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October.

  • parser: fixed, fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Issue closed.
  • hint: won't fix, the booking-v3 form is retired. Issue closed.
  • one-day move: not covered by either. Its issue stays open.

How the reports were placed

  • The export pages overlap: some reports come twice. One id, one report.
  • A report's cause comes from its tenant (locale and booking form, locales.ts), not from intake's tag. Intake guessed from the reporter's browser, and its hint tags are wrong both ways: the tagged as column shows it.
  • Subjects say nothing about cause (decision 0001 merges on them). All three problems share subjects such as "Booking on the wrong date".

Since April

Lessons

  • A count is not a cause. Before marking a big ticket known, read its reports against where they came from.
  • Merging on subject puts different bugs in one thread. Split them before the fix ships.
